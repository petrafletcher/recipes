---
title: "Chicken Doner Kebab"
description: "Chicken thighs marinated overnight in lemon, garlic and warm spices, cooked on a rotisserie over wood smoke and sliced into flatbreads with salad."
source: "Love2BBQ"
source_url: "https://www.love2bbq.co.uk/chicken-doner-kebab/"
image: "https://www.love2bbq.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2018/06/Chicken-Doner-Kebab-1920.jpg"
prep_time: 20
cook_time: 75
total_time: 95
labels: [turkish, main, chicken, bbq, takeaway-style, make-ahead]
ingredients:
  - "2 lb boneless and skinless chicken thighs"
  - "3 tbsp tomato puree"
  - "4 garlic cloves (minced)"
  - "2 lemons (zested and juiced)"
  - "2 tsp onion powder"
  - "2 tsp cumin"
  - "½ tsp ground allspice"
  - "1 tbsp thyme (dried)"
  - "1 tbsp salt"
  - "2 tsp black pepper"
  - "8 tbsp olive oil (extra virgin)"
  - "1 tbsp Angus & Oink Shawarma Rub or Smokey Carter Greek Gyros Rub (optional)"
method:
  - "Stir together everything except the chicken in a bowl to make the marinade."
  - "Put the chicken in a large bowl, add the marinade and toss until every piece is coated. Cover and chill overnight."
  - "Take the chicken out of the fridge about 20 minutes before cooking, then thread it onto the rotisserie spit."
  - "Get the barbecue up to 180–200°C / 350–400°F. Put the chicken on and add 3–4 chunks of cherry wood for smoke and colour."
  - "Cook for roughly 1 hour 15 minutes, starting to check the internal temperature after 45–60 minutes. It's done at 75°C / 167°F."
  - "Lift off the barbecue and rest for 5–10 minutes."
  - "Slide the meat off the spit, chop it roughly and serve in flatbreads or pittas with salad."
