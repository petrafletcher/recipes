---
title: "Hunters Chicken"
description: "A British pub classic: spiced chicken breasts wrapped in streaky bacon, baked, then topped with BBQ sauce and melted cheese. Quick enough for a weeknight."
source: "Don't Go Bacon My Heart"
source_url: "https://www.dontgobaconmyheart.co.uk/hunters-chicken/"
image: "https://www.dontgobaconmyheart.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/hunters-chicken.jpg"
prep_time: 5
cook_time: 30
total_time: 35
servings: "4"
labels: [british, main, chicken, oven, family-friendly, quick]
ingredients:
  - group: "Hunters chicken"
    items:
      - "4 boneless/skinless chicken breasts (around 7oz/200g each)"
      - "12 slices of streaky bacon"
      - "1 cup / 240ml good quality BBQ sauce"
      - "1 cup / 100g grated cheese, or as needed to cover chicken"
      - "finely diced fresh chives, to garnish (optional)"
  - group: "Chicken seasoning"
    items:
      - "1 tsp smoked paprika"
      - "1/2 tsp garlic powder"
      - "1/4 tsp each: salt, black pepper, cayenne pepper (can omit cayenne)"
method:
  - "Heat the oven to 200°C / 400°F."
  - "Mix the seasoning in a small bowl and scatter it evenly over both sides of each chicken breast. Don't rub it in, or it will clump."
  - "Wrap each breast in 3 bacon slices and set them in a cast-iron skillet or shallow baking dish with the bacon ends tucked underneath."
  - "Bake for 25 minutes, until the chicken is piping hot and white all the way through."
  - "Take the dish out and switch the oven to grill. Spoon plenty of BBQ sauce over each breast and pile on the cheese."
  - "Grill until the cheese has melted, then scatter with chives, if using, and serve."
