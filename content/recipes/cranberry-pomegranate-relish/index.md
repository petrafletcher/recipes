---
title: "Cranberry & Pomegranate Relish"
description: "A cranberry sauce with shallot, pomegranate juice, orange and brown sugar, finished with pomegranate seeds. Serve it with turkey or pork, or spread it in leftover sandwiches."
source: "Nugget Markets"
source_url: "https://www.nuggetmarket.com/recipes/781/cranberry-pomegranate-relish/"
prep_time: 5
cook_time: 25
total_time: 30
servings: "About 2 cups"
labels: [american, sauce, side, vegetarian, stovetop, christmas, make-ahead]
ingredients:
  - "2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil"
  - "1 shallot, sliced thin"
  - "10 ounces cranberries, frozen"
  - "1 cup pomegranate juice"
  - "⅔ cup brown sugar"
  - "¼ cup orange juice"
  - "½ cup pomegranate arils"
method:
  - "Warm the olive oil in a small saucepan over medium heat."
  - "Add the shallot and cook for about 5 minutes, until soft and translucent."
  - "Tip in the cranberries, pomegranate juice, brown sugar and orange juice, and let it simmer for 20 minutes."
  - "Take the pan off the heat and stir through the pomegranate arils."
  - "Serve warm or at room temperature."
---
