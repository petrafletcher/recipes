---
title: "Chicken Tikka Masala"
description: "Chicken marinated in spiced yogurt, charred in the oven, air fryer or a skillet, then simmered in a creamy onion and tomato masala. The curry-house favourite made at home."
source: "Swasthi's Recipes"
source_url: "https://www.indianhealthyrecipes.com/chicken-tikka-masala/"
image: "https://www.indianhealthyrecipes.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/chicken-tikka-masala.jpg"
prep_time: 20
cook_time: 40
total_time: 60
servings: "6"
labels: [indian, main, chicken, oven, stovetop, curry, takeaway-style, make-ahead]
ingredients:
  - group: "Marinade"
    items:
      - "1½ lbs (700 grams) boneless chicken (preferably thighs, or breasts)"
      - "½ cup (120 ml) Greek yogurt (hung curd)"
      - "½ to 1 teaspoon Kashmiri red chili powder"
      - "1 teaspoon garam masala"
      - "¼ teaspoon turmeric"
      - "1 teaspoon coriander powder"
      - "½ teaspoon cumin powder"
      - "½ teaspoon salt"
      - "1 tablespoon lemon juice"
      - "1 tablespoon kasuri methi"
      - "1 tablespoon ginger garlic paste"
      - "1 tablespoon oil (mustard oil if you have)"
  - group: "Tikka masala"
    items:
      - "3 tablespoons oil (or ghee)"
      - "1½ cups (180 grams) onions, finely chopped"
      - "1 green chilli (optional)"
      - "1 teaspoon salt"
      - "1 tablespoon ginger garlic paste"
      - "½ to 1 teaspoon Kashmiri red chili powder"
      - "2 teaspoons garam masala"
      - "1 tablespoon coriander powder"
      - "1 to 1½ teaspoons cumin powder"
      - "1 to 2 teaspoons sugar"
      - "1 lb (450 grams) tomatoes, pureed/chopped (or 1¼ cups passata, or a 14 oz can of peeled tomatoes)"
      - "1 cup hot water"
      - "½ tablespoon kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves)"
      - "½ cup heavy cream/whipping cream (or ⅓ cup whole raw cashews)"
  - group: "Garnish"
    items:
      - "3 tablespoons heavy cream"
      - "3 tablespoons coriander leaves, finely chopped"
method:
  - group: "Marinate the chicken"
    steps:
      - "Cut the chicken into 1–1½ inch cubes and pat dry with kitchen paper."
      - "Combine every marinade ingredient in a bowl, using thick yogurt so it doesn't turn watery, and leave out the lemon juice if your yogurt is sour. Coat the chicken well, cover and refrigerate for 8–48 hours (at least 3 hours if pushed for time)."
  - group: "Make the masala"
    steps:
      - "Heat the oil or ghee in a pan over medium heat. Add the onions and salt and fry for 10–12 minutes until deep golden, not pale."
      - "Add the green chilli and ginger garlic paste and fry for 40–60 seconds until aromatic. Turn the heat to low, add the chilli powder, garam masala, coriander and cumin powders, and stir quickly so they don't burn."
      - "Straight away, add the tomatoes and cook over medium-high heat, stirring often, until thick. Fresh tomatoes take 7–8 minutes and passata about 2."
      - "Pour in the cup of water, boiling hot, and bring back to a rolling boil. Cover and simmer on low for 12–14 minutes until thick and fragrant. If you like, cool slightly, blend smooth and return it to the pan."
  - group: "Grill the chicken"
    steps:
      - "While the sauce simmers, heat the oven to 240°C / 460°F. Thread the chicken onto metal or soaked bamboo skewers and set them on a prepared tray on the middle shelf."
      - "Bake for 9–10 minutes, turn, and bake another 9–10 minutes. Finish under the grill (broiler) for 2–3 minutes until lightly charred."
      - "Or air fry at 200°C / 400°F for 6 minutes per side, basting with leftover marinade and giving it 2 more minutes if it looks dry. Or sear in a hot, oiled heavy pan over medium-high heat for 3–4 minutes per side, in batches, until charred and almost cooked through."
  - group: "Finish"
    steps:
      - "Stir the sugar and cream (or cashew paste) into the sauce and cook for a few minutes until thick and creamy. Loosen with a little hot water if it's too thick."
      - "Add the chicken and crushed kasuri methi. Simmer for 2–3 minutes until the chicken is tender, then taste and adjust the salt, garam masala or sugar."
      - "Turn off the heat, swirl over the garnish cream and scatter with coriander. Serve with naan, roti or rice."
---

The total time doesn't include marinating. The original suggests up to 8 hours. Serve with [butter naan]({{< relref "recipes/butter-naan" >}}).
