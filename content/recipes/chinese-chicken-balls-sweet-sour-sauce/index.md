title: "Chinese Chicken Balls with Sweet and Sour Sauce"
description: "Takeaway-style chicken balls in a light, crisp batter, served with a homemade ketchup and vinegar sweet and sour sauce."
source: "Food.com (Kittencal)"
source_url: "https://www.food.com/recipe/kittencals-chinese-chicken-balls-with-sweet-and-sour-sauce-132546"
image: "https://img.sndimg.com/food/image/upload/q_92,fl_progressive,w_1200,c_scale/v1/img/recipes/13/25/46/uNGG7ogZTxmcgvA1WlUD_sweet-and-sour-chicken-chinese-1811.jpg"
prep_time: 25
cook_time: 5
total_time: 30
servings: "8"
labels: [chinese, main, snack, chicken, fry, takeaway-style, family-friendly]
ingredients:
  - group: "Sweet and sour sauce"
    items:
      - "1/2 cup ketchup"
      - "1 teaspoon soy sauce"
      - "1/3 cup white vinegar"
      - "1/2 cup brown sugar, lightly packed"
      - "1 cup white sugar (can reduce by 2-3 tablespoons for a less sweet taste)"
      - "3/4 cup cold water (or unsweetened pineapple juice, or half each)"
      - "3 tablespoons cornstarch (2 for a thinner sauce, 4 for a very thick sauce)"
  - group: "Chicken balls"
    items:
      - "1 cup all-purpose flour"
      - "1 cup cornstarch"
      - "2 teaspoons baking powder"
      - "2 teaspoons baking soda"
      - "1/2 teaspoon garlic powder (garlic lovers add 1 teaspoon)"
      - "2 teaspoons sugar"
      - "1 1/3 cups cold water"
      - "1-2 teaspoons sesame oil (optional)"
      - "oil (for frying)"
      - "8 chicken breasts, cut into bite-size pieces"
      - "seasoning salt (optional)"
method:
  - group: "Sweet and sour sauce"
    steps:
      - "Stir the ketchup, soy sauce, vinegar, brown sugar and white sugar together in a saucepan."
      - "In a cup, whisk the cold water (or pineapple juice) with the cornstarch until smooth, then whisk it into the pan."
      - "Over medium-high heat, stir constantly until the sauce boils and bubbles, then lower to medium-low and keep stirring for a few minutes until thickened."
      - "Take off the heat and leave at room temperature while you cook the chicken (or cool and refrigerate until needed)."
  - group: "Chicken balls"
    steps:
      - "In a large bowl, mix the flour, cornstarch, baking powder, baking soda and garlic powder."
      - "Add the cold water and sesame oil, if using, and whisk hard until smooth. Tip in the chicken and mix with clean hands until every piece is coated."
      - "Heat the oil in a deep fryer or Dutch oven to 190°C / 375°F."
      - "Carefully lower the battered chicken into the oil and fry for 3–4 minutes until golden brown."
      - "Drain on paper towels and sprinkle with seasoning salt straight away, if you like. Serve hot with the sauce."
