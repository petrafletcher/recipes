---
title: "The Best Pulled Pork in a Crock Pot"
description: "Pork shoulder is rubbed with a paprika, honey and red wine vinegar paste and cooked low and slow over onion until it falls apart. It uses no ketchup or refined sugar."
source: "100 Days of Real Food"
source_url: "https://www.100daysofrealfood.com/recipe-best-pulled-pork-crock-pot/"
image: "https://www.100daysofrealfood.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/12/pulled-pork-scaled.jpeg"
prep_time: 5
cook_time: 420
total_time: 425
servings: "6"
labels: [american, main, pork, slow-cooker, family-friendly, make-ahead]
ingredients:
  - "3 tablespoons paprika"
  - "1 tablespoon kosher salt"
  - "2 teaspoons pepper"
  - "½ teaspoon cayenne pepper"
  - "1 teaspoon garlic powder"
  - "½ teaspoon thyme (dried)"
  - "½ cup honey"
  - "¼ cup red wine vinegar"
  - "3 tablespoons olive oil"
  - "1 onion (peeled and cut in half)"
  - "3-5 pounds pork shoulder (cut in half)"
method:
  - "Stir the paprika, salt, pepper, cayenne, garlic powder and thyme together in a medium bowl with a fork."
  - "Add the honey, red wine vinegar and olive oil and mix to a thick paste."
  - "Put the onion halves in the base of the slow cooker and set the two pieces of pork on them. Coat the pork on every side with the paste; it doesn't matter if some runs down into the pot."
  - "Cover and cook on low for 7–8 hours, until the meat pulls apart easily with a fork."
  - "Shred the pork and serve warm. Chill or freeze any leftovers."
