---
title: "Apple-Stuffed Pork Tenderloin au Jus"
description: "Pork tenderloins are stuffed with caramelised red onion, apple, rum-soaked cherries and sage, then seared and roasted. An apple cider and beef broth jus goes alongside."
source: "Nugget Markets"
source_url: "https://www.nuggetmarket.com/recipes/272/apple-stuffed-pork-tenderloin-au-jus/"
prep_time: 30
cook_time: 75
total_time: 105
servings: "4–6"
labels: [american, main, pork, oven, stovetop, christmas]
ingredients:
  - "2 cups fresh apple cider"
  - "1 cup beef broth"
  - "1 tablespoon unsalted butter"
  - "1 red onion, sliced thin"
  - "2 Granny Smith apples, small-diced"
  - "1 cup dried cherries"
  - "1 cup rum"
  - "¼ cup panko breadcrumbs"
  - "4 fresh sage leaves, chiffonade"
  - "2 pork tenderloins, trimmed"
  - "2 tablespoons canola oil"
method:
  - group: "Jus base"
    steps:
      - "Boil the apple cider hard until it turns syrupy and has dropped to about ¼ cup. Take it off the heat, whisk in the beef broth and leave it to cool."
  - group: "Stuffing"
    steps:
      - "Melt the butter in a large frying pan over medium-low heat. Cook the sliced onion slowly, stirring now and then, for about 30 minutes until deeply caramelised."
      - "Stir in the diced apple and dried cherries. Take the pan off the heat before pouring in the rum, then put it back on and cook until the rum has almost all evaporated."
      - "Mix in the panko and sage, take the pan off the heat and let the filling cool to room temperature."
  - group: "Pork"
    steps:
      - "Butterfly each tenderloin and open it out. Lay plastic wrap over the top and pound the meat flat, then remove the wrap."
      - "Spread the cooled filling over the pork, keeping a ½-inch (1 cm) border clear. Roll each one up tightly from a long side and tie with kitchen string at roughly 2-inch (5 cm) intervals."
      - "Heat the oven to 175°C / 350°F."
      - "Heat the canola oil in a large ovenproof frying pan over medium-high heat. Brown the rolls on three sides, 1–2 minutes a side, then turn them so the unbrowned side faces down."
      - "Move the pan to the oven and roast for about 35–40 minutes, until the centre of the pork reads 60–63°C / 140–145°F."
      - "Lift the rolls onto a board and cover them loosely with foil to rest."
      - "Set the pan over medium heat, pour in the cooled cider and broth mixture, and scrape up the browned bits from the bottom to make the jus."
      - "Slice the pork and spoon the jus over it."
---

The original bookmark was the "Apple Hill" stuffed pork loin. Nugget Markets has since replaced it with this tenderloin version, which uses the same stuffing.
