---
title: "Homemade BBQ Sauce"
description: "A sweet, smoky, tangy barbecue sauce of ketchup, molasses, brown sugar and cider vinegar, blended smooth and finished with butter."
source: "Chef Billy Parisi"
source_url: "https://www.billyparisi.com/homemade-bbq-sauce-recipe/"
image: "https://www.billyparisi.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/sweet-bbq-sauce-fb.jpg"
prep_time: 5
cook_time: 20
total_time: 25
servings: "About 4 cups"
labels: [american, sauce, vegetarian, stovetop, bbq, make-ahead]
ingredients:
  - "1 tablespoon oil"
  - "½ peeled small diced yellow onion"
  - "2 finely minced garlic cloves"
  - "2 cups ketchup"
  - "2/3 cup apple cider vinegar"
  - "¼ cup molasses"
  - "1 cup packed light brown sugar"
  - "1 tablespoon dry mustard"
  - "1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper"
  - "1/2 teaspoon black pepper"
  - "1 teaspoon salt"
  - "1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce"
  - "1 stick unsalted butter"
  - "2 teaspoons liquid smoke (optional)"
method:
  - "Warm the oil in a medium saucepan over low heat. Add the onion and cook gently, stirring now and then, for 15–20 minutes until it is deeply browned."
  - "Stir in the garlic and give it 1–2 minutes, until you can smell it."
  - "Add the ketchup, vinegar, molasses, brown sugar, Worcestershire sauce, dry mustard, cayenne, black pepper, salt and the liquid smoke if using. Stir thoroughly and bring up to a gentle simmer."
  - "Let it bubble over low to medium heat for 5–10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it has thickened."
  - "Blend on low speed with a stick or jug blender just until completely smooth. Over-blending can lighten the colour and change the texture."
  - "Take the pan off the heat and whisk in the butter until melted and the sauce looks glossy."
  - "Use straight away or make up to 3 days ahead. Keep covered in the fridge for up to 10 days and reheat gently over low heat. It doesn't freeze well."
