---
title: "Chicken Katsu Curry"
description: "Panko-crumbed chicken breasts fried until crisp and served with a mild, slightly sweet Japanese curry sauce, rice and salad. Gizzi Erskine's recipe."
source: "The Happy Foodie"
source_url: "https://thehappyfoodie.co.uk/recipes/chicken-katsu-curry/"
image: "https://thehappyfoodie.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/chicken-katsu-curry_s900x0_c2469x1442_l0x808.png"
servings: "4"
labels: [japanese, main, chicken, fry, stovetop, curry, takeaway-style]
ingredients:
  - group: "Chicken"
    items:
      - "100g flour, seasoned with lots of salt and pepper"
      - "1 free-range egg, lightly beaten"
      - "200g Japanese panko breadcrumbs"
      - "4 boneless chicken breasts"
      - "100ml groundnut or vegetable oil"
      - "steamed rice and salad to serve"
  - group: "Curry sauce"
    items:
      - "1 tbsp groundnut or vegetable oil"
      - "1 onion, peeled and chopped"
      - "5 whole garlic cloves, peeled"
      - "2 carrots, peeled and chopped"
      - "2 tbsp plain flour"
      - "1 tbsp medium curry powder"
      - "600ml chicken stock"
      - "2 tsp honey"
      - "1 tbsp soy sauce"
      - "1 bay leaf"
      - "1/2 tsp garam masala"
method:
  - group: "Curry sauce"
    steps:
      - "Warm the oil in a small pan and fry the onion and garlic for 2 minutes."
      - "Add the carrots, cover and cook gently for about 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until soft and beginning to caramelise."
      - "Stir in the flour and curry powder and cook for 1 minute."
      - "Pour in the stock a little at a time, stirring as you go to avoid lumps. Add the honey, soy sauce and bay leaf."
      - "Bring to the boil, then turn down and simmer slowly for 20 minutes until thickened and full of flavour but still pourable."
      - "Stir in the garam masala. Sieve the sauce if you want it smooth, or leave it chunky."
  - group: "Chicken"
    steps:
      - "Put the seasoned flour, beaten egg and panko on three separate plates. Coat each breast in flour, then egg, then press into the panko."
      - "Heat the oil in a frying pan and fry the chicken for 5 minutes on each side until golden and cooked through."
      - "Lift out with a slotted spoon and drain on kitchen paper."
      - "Slice on the diagonal, spoon over the sauce and serve with steamed rice and salad."
