---
title: "Butter Chicken (Chicken Makhani)"
description: "Chicken given two marinades, the second in spiced yogurt, then folded into a mild, velvety tomato and cashew sauce with butter, kasuri methi and cream. Serve with naan or rice."
source: "Swasthi's Recipes"
source_url: "https://www.indianhealthyrecipes.com/butter-chicken/"
image: "https://www.indianhealthyrecipes.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/butter-chicken-recipe.jpg"
prep_time: 25
cook_time: 30
servings: "4"
labels: [indian, main, chicken, stovetop, curry, takeaway-style, make-ahead]
ingredients:
  - group: "First marinade"
    items:
      - "½ kg (1.1 lbs) boneless chicken (cut to 1 inch pieces)"
      - "½ to ¾ teaspoon Kashmiri red chili powder (or paprika, reduce for kids)"
      - "¼ to ⅓ teaspoon salt"
      - "¾ to 1 tablespoon lemon juice"
  - group: "Second marinade"
    items:
      - "⅓ cup Greek yogurt (hung curd/thick curd)"
      - "¾ tablespoon ginger garlic paste"
      - "⅛ teaspoon turmeric (optional)"
      - "¾ to 1 teaspoon garam masala"
      - "½ teaspoon cumin powder (optional)"
      - "1 teaspoon coriander powder (optional)"
      - "1 teaspoon kasuri methi (optional, dried fenugreek leaves)"
      - "¾ to 1 tablespoon oil"
  - group: "Sauce"
    items:
      - "2 to 3 tablespoons butter or ghee (divided)"
      - "2 inch cinnamon piece (optional)"
      - "2 to 4 green cardamoms (optional)"
      - "2 to 4 cloves (optional)"
      - "1½ cups (130 grams) onions, sliced (optional)"
      - "600 grams (1.3 lbs) fresh tomatoes (or 1 cup passata, or ⅓ cup double concentrate tomato paste)"
      - "¾ tablespoon ginger garlic paste"
      - "1 to 2 green chilies (optional)"
      - "28 (42 grams, ⅓ cup) whole raw cashews, soaked in hot water (or ¼ to ⅓ cup heavy cream)"
      - "½ cup water to blend (more if required)"
      - "1 to 2 teaspoons Kashmiri chili powder"
      - "1 to 1½ teaspoons garam masala (divided)"
      - "1 to 1½ teaspoons coriander powder (optional)"
      - "½ teaspoon cumin powder (optional)"
      - "½ to ¾ teaspoon salt"
      - "1 teaspoon sugar"
      - "½ tablespoon kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves)"
      - "1 cup hot water (½ cup if omitting cashews)"
      - "⅓ cup (80 to 100 ml) heavy cream or whipping cream (divided)"
      - "2 tablespoons coriander leaves, finely chopped, to garnish"
method:
  - group: "Marinate the chicken"
    steps:
      - "Toss the chicken with the first-marinade chilli powder, salt and lemon juice. Cover and leave for 20 minutes."
      - "Add every second-marinade ingredient and mix well. Cover and chill for at least 30 minutes. Overnight (around 12 hours) gives the most tender result, and don't go beyond 48 hours."
  - group: "Make the sauce"
    steps:
      - "If using onions, fry them in 1 tablespoon of oil for about 7 minutes until lightly golden, then let them cool."
      - "Blend the tomatoes, onions, cashews and ½ cup water until very smooth, adding a splash more water if needed. Strain it if it isn't smooth."
      - "Take the chicken out of the fridge so it can come close to room temperature."
      - "Melt 2 tablespoons of the butter in a pan and add the cinnamon, cardamoms and cloves if using. When they sizzle, add the ginger garlic paste and green chillies. Stir over low to medium heat for 1–2 minutes until fragrant but not browned."
      - "Turn off the heat. Stir in the chilli powder, cumin, coriander powder and most of the garam masala, then add the purée."
      - "Partly cover and bring to a boil over medium-high heat. Lower the heat and cook, stirring often, for 12–15 minutes until thick."
      - "Pour in the 1 cup of hot water and simmer, partly covered, for about 10 minutes, until the sauce thickens and fat shows on the surface. Pick out the whole spices if you like."
  - group: "Cook the chicken and finish"
    steps:
      - "Meanwhile, heat 1 tablespoon of the butter or ghee in a pan or griddle (not nonstick). Lay the chicken in a single, spaced-out layer and cook over medium heat for about 2 minutes. Turn it and keep cooking until the marinade has dried up, then raise the heat to brown it on both sides. Work in 2–3 batches and watch it doesn't burn. It will finish cooking in the sauce."
      - "Alternatively, bake the chicken on skewers or a greased tray at 240°C / 460°F for 20–22 minutes, turning halfway. Preheat the oven for at least 15 minutes first."
      - "Add the chicken to the sauce, with up to ½ cup more hot water if it is too thick. Cover and simmer for 5–7 minutes until the chicken is tender."
      - "Stir in the salt, sugar and crushed kasuri methi. Taste, then adjust with the rest of the garam masala and an extra knob of butter if you like."
      - "Turn off the heat. Mix a few spoonfuls of the hot sauce into most of the cream so it doesn't curdle, then stir it into the pan. Finish with the coriander and a drizzle of the remaining cream, and serve with naan or rice."
---

Plan ahead: the original recommends marinating the chicken for up to 12 hours. Serve with [butter naan]({{< relref "recipes/butter-naan" >}}).
