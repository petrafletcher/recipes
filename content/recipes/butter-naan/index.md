---
title: "Butter Naan"
description: "Soft garlic butter naan from a yogurt and milk dough raised with baking powder instead of yeast. It's cooked on a hot skillet, so you don't need a tandoor."
source: "Swasthi's Recipes"
source_url: "https://www.indianhealthyrecipes.com/naan/"
image: "https://www.indianhealthyrecipes.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/butter-naan.jpg"
prep_time: 15
cook_time: 15
total_time: 30
servings: "6"
labels: [indian, bread, side, vegetarian, stovetop, takeaway-style]
ingredients:
  - group: "Dough"
    items:
      - "2 cups (250 grams) all-purpose flour (or 1 cup wheat flour + 1 cup all-purpose flour)"
      - "½ cup (120 ml) plain yogurt (curd)"
      - "6 to 8 tablespoons lukewarm milk (may need more)"
      - "2 tablespoons oil or melted butter"
      - "2 teaspoons sugar"
      - "½ to ¾ teaspoon salt"
      - "1 to 1½ teaspoons grated garlic (optional)"
  - group: "Leavening (or use ¾ tsp instant yeast)"
    items:
      - "½ teaspoon baking powder"
      - "¼ teaspoon baking soda"
  - group: "Toppings"
    items:
      - "5 to 6 garlic cloves, sliced or chopped"
      - "3 tablespoons melted butter"
      - "¼ cup coriander leaves, finely chopped"
      - "1 tsp kalonji seeds (optional)"
method:
  - "In a large bowl, combine the flour, baking powder, baking soda, sugar, salt and grated garlic."
  - "Add the yogurt, oil or butter and about 4 tablespoons of the milk. Mix to a dough, adding more milk a little at a time as needed."
  - "Knead until soft, elastic and not sticky. A fingertip pressed into it should leave a dent easily. Don't over-knead or the naan will be tough."
  - "Cover and rest at room temperature for 30 minutes to 2 hours, or cook right away. If you're using yeast instead, leave it somewhere warm for about 2 hours until doubled, then knock it back."
  - "With greased hands, divide into 6–8 smooth balls and keep them covered. Get the toppings ready: chop the garlic and coriander and melt the butter."
  - "On a very lightly floured surface, stretch each ball by hand into an oval about 8–9 inches long and 5–6 inches wide, keeping it fairly thick. Scatter over garlic, kalonji and coriander and press them in lightly."
  - "Heat a cast-iron skillet or tawa until hot but not smoking. Lay a naan in it. Bubbles should form within about a minute, and covering the pan helps it puff."
  - "Flip with tongs and cook until both sides have brown spots and a little char, a few minutes per side. You can finish it over a gas flame. Don't press it down."
  - "Brush with melted butter and serve hot."
---
