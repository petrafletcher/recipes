---
title: "Sage Orange Christmas Chicken"
description: "A whole chicken with orange, lemon, garlic and herb butter under the skin, roasted on citrus, onion and garlic with white wine. The pan juices make a buttery sauce."
source: "Simply Delicious"
source_url: "https://simply-delicious-food.com/sage-orange-christmas-chicken/"
image: "https://simply-delicious-food.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/Roasted-Christmas-Chicken-5.jpg"
prep_time: 20
cook_time: 60
total_time: 80
servings: "6"
labels: [main, chicken, oven, roast, christmas]
ingredients:
  - group: "Herb butter"
    items:
      - "½ cup softened butter"
      - "zest of 1 orange"
      - "zest of 1 lemon"
      - "2 garlic cloves finely minced/crushed"
      - "1 tbsp sage finely chopped"
      - "2 tsp thyme leaves"
      - "1 tsp salt"
      - "½ tsp pepper"
  - group: "Chicken and roasting pan"
    items:
      - "1.5 kg / 3lb whole chicken"
      - "2 tbsp olive oil"
      - "salt and pepper to taste"
      - "2 oranges halved"
      - "1 onion quartered"
      - "2 garlic bulbs halved"
      - "10 sprigs fresh thyme"
      - "6 sage leaves"
      - "1 cup white wine"
method:
  - group: "Herb butter"
    steps:
      - "Combine the softened butter with the orange and lemon zest, garlic, chopped sage, thyme leaves, salt and pepper, and beat until evenly mixed."
  - group: "Chicken"
    steps:
      - "Take the chicken out of the fridge in time for it to reach room temperature. Heat the oven to 180°C / 350°F."
      - "Work your fingers under the skin of the breast and drumsticks to loosen it, being careful not to tear it. Push the herb butter underneath and smooth it over the meat from the outside so it spreads evenly."
      - "Scatter the orange halves, onion quarters, halved garlic bulbs, thyme sprigs and sage leaves over the base of a roasting dish and sit the chicken on top."
      - "Rub the chicken with the olive oil, season it well all over with salt and pepper, and pour the wine into the dish around it."
      - "Roast for 45–60 minutes, depending on size, until golden and cooked through: 72°C / 165°F on a meat thermometer, or juices that run clear when you cut between the breast and drumstick. You can take it out at about 65°C / 150°F, as the temperature keeps rising while it rests."
      - "Cover and rest for at least 10 minutes (20 is better) before carving. Serve with the buttery pan juices."
